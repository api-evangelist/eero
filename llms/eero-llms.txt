About eero
eero invented home mesh wifi and continues to refine it. Their systems are designed to be simple to set up, powerful enough for demanding households and businesses, secure at the router level, and reliably consistent throughout a space.

eero devices power homes and businesses across 27+ countries and have earned an average 4.5-star rating across Amazon reviews. The eero app is award-winning.

Products
eero Signal — Designed to eliminate internet outages.
eero Pro 7 — Wi-Fi 7 performance for high-speed, high-demand environments.
eero PoE 7 — Wi-Fi 7 with Power over Ethernet support, going above and beyond standard deployments.
eero Plus — A subscription service offering enhanced security, multi-network management, and greater control from anywhere.
Key Features
Simple Setup
Set up a Wi-Fi network in minutes with placement recommendations for optimal performance and coverage.

Automatic Updates
Software updates deliver new features, performance improvements, and security enhancements without interrupting wifi service.

Security Innovation
Unlike traditional security software that must be installed and updated on each device individually, eero's router-level protection automatically secures every connected device on the network.

Wifi Scheduling
Limit screen time across individual devices or an entire home network — useful for minimizing distractions at dinner, during homework, or before bed.

Instant Guest Wifi
Easily create a guest network and share access via a link or QR code.

eero Plus Subscription
Provides even more control and enhanced security, making it fast and easy to manage multiple networks and devices from anywhere.
